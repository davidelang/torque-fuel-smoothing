# Antigravity

Law: `AGENTS.md`. `GEMINI.md` is a symlink to it, and every Antigravity model loads it. Role: your launcher prompt (`roles/<role>.md`).

Tool names: `run_shell_command` is the shell. `write_to_file` writes a file. `replace_file_content` and `multi_replace_file_content` edit. `invoke_subagent` spawns a subagent.
